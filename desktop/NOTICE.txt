Black Marble 2025 VJ146A4 V2 app subsets are included (540 tiles, about 1.14 GB).
Native-resolution radiance, quality and coordinates are retained losslessly.
These are derived app subsets, not the complete NASA HDF-EOS products.
DEM is downloaded from the public Copernicus GLO-90 store for the selected region.
